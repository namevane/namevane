    A list can point names on this computer at the addresses in the file, including names you did not type. Sharing a list with your own team is what this is for. A list from someone you do not know can send your bank, your mail, or any other site to an address they control. Namevane does not review lists, and the people who make it are not responsible for the addresses in a list you add. Getting around blocking in a country is not what Namevane is for.

    A downloaded list is saved here. Press Apply on Addresses to use it on this computer. A name you added yourself is used instead of the same name in a list.
